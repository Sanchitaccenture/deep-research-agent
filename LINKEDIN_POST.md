## Hashtag strategy

Use 3-5 hashtags max. Best mix for AI engineering roles:

- `#AI` — huge reach
- `#LangGraph` or `#RAG` — technical specificity
- `#FullStack` — broadens your audience
- `#Python` / `#React` — for keyword-search recruiters
- `#OpenToWork` — only if you actually are

## Tagging

Tag @LangChainAI, @vercel, @groq (they sometimes reshare) — but only if
you're comfortable with the visibility.

## After posting

- **First 30 min matters most** for LinkedIn's algorithm. Ping 5 friends
  on Slack/WhatsApp to like/comment early.
- Reply to every comment within 6 hours — signals engagement to the feed.
- **Don't edit the post** for the first hour — LinkedIn penalises edits.
- Cross-post to Reddit (`r/MachineLearning`, `r/LocalLLaMA`) 24h later —
  never simultaneously.
